Processing & Preparation Workflow

Rational Sequence
  1. 1 Cook Starch Gel: Whisk 18g cornflour into 132ml cold water. Simmer on induction for 60–90s until clear and gelatinised. Let cool 5m.
  2. 2 Vegan Buttermilk: Whisk 18ml cider vinegar into 540ml soy milk. Rest 5m until curdled. Whisk in warm plant butter, cooled starch gel, and vanilla.
  3. 3 Dry Aeration & Stir: Sift flour, sugar, baking powder, bicarb, and salt into bowl. Add wet mix. Stir on Low for 25–30s max; leave lumpy.
  4. 4 Rest & Tray Setup: Rest batter 12–15m to develop bubble matrix. Spray twelve 10.2cm seamless rings on silicone mats over 1/1 GN grids.
  5. 5 Portion & Combi Bake: Scale 135g batter per ring (~1/3 height). Drop-tap tray twice on bench to level. Bake in Rational (152°C, 50% Steam, Fan 2) for 12–13m (Core: 92°C–94°C). Lift rings immediately; cool 20m[cite: 1].

LULA Service Reheat & Cross-Contamination Standard

Ambient / Chilled (90s): Flash at 200°C for 90s on fresh parchment, or sear on flat-top in 5g plant butter under cloche (45s/side)[cite: 1].
From Frozen (3–3.5m): Brush top with plant butter; bake at 200°C on dedicated clean parchment to eliminate dairy contamination[cite: 1].
